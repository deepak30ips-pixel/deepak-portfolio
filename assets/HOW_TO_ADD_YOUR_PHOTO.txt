HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTO:
---------------------
1. Choose any portrait / profile photo of yourself.
2. Rename your photo file to: profile.jpg
3. Paste it directly into this "assets" folder:
   C:\Users\PC\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\deepak-portfolio\assets\profile.jpg

Your portfolio will instantly display your photo in the Hero section with the glowing gradient border and floating badges!
